How I Work

Source → Insight

  1. Discover 01

    Profile source systems, interview stakeholders, and map what the business actually needs from the data.

  2. Define 02

    Source-to-target mappings, data models, fact and dimension design, SCD strategy and quality rules.

  3. Design 03

    Build ETL/ELT pipelines in ADF, Databricks and Spark, with validation, reconciliation and unit tests.

  4. Deliver 04

    CI/CD releases through Azure DevOps, monitoring, root-cause analysis and tuning based on real run metrics.
